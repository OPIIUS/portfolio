Verification standards

Placement can be paid. Verification is earned.

The OPIIUS Verified badge means we've checked a rental agency ourselves. Here is exactly what we check, how often, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Checks

What we check

Every Verified rental agency passes these checks, through an OPIIUS visit or a video walkthrough.

CategoryChecks
Car & bike rentalsBusiness proof (trade licence, Udyam or GST) · Owner's photo ID · Rent-a-cab or rent-a-motorcycle permit · RC and commercial insurance for every listed vehicle · Photos of the actual fleet

Rules that keep it honest

  • Re-checked every six months. The badge shows the date of the last check.
  • Never sold. The Verified fee covers our checks; agencies that don't pass get a full refund.
  • Featured is labelled. Paid placement always says Featured and is limited to 3 per category per city.
  • Real reviews only. Reviews come only from customers who sent an inquiry through OPIIUS, tied to its reference.
  • Complaints count. Two unresolved complaints suspend the badge until we review them.

What a verified inquiry is

Every request sent through OPIIUS carries a reference number (like OP-261002-K3F9Q) and the customer's car, dates and pickup point, sent from their own WhatsApp number. Agencies see real, specific requests, and customers can quote the reference if anything goes wrong.
