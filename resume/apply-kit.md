## 6. The routine that actually works

- **10 applications a day.** Not 3 perfect ones. Volume is the whole game at this stage.
- **Portfolio link in every single one**, including the LinkedIn headline.
- **Apply within 24 hours of a posting.** Older than a week is mostly wasted effort.
- **Offer a paid trial task.** It beats a CV every time when you have no formal experience.
- **Track it.** Company, date, link, reply. If 30 applications get 0 replies, the message is wrong —
  not you.
