FAQ

Before you book.

Do I need my G1 to start lessons?

Yes. In Ontario you need a G1 licence to drive with an instructor. Book your first lesson as soon as you have it.

Can I use your car for my road test?

Yes. Choose a road test package and we bring the car, with a warm-up lesson before your test.

Can you help me get an earlier road test?

Yes. Pick "Early road test booking help" in the form and tell us your current date.

How do I pay?

Your instructor will confirm payment options when your lesson is booked.
