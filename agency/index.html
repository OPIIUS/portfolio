Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Usually you can keep your current business number. We set up the official WhatsApp Business API on it for you. If you'd rather keep your personal chats separate, we help you get a second number.

What happens when the bot doesn't know the answer?

It doesn't guess. It tells the customer a person will reply, and alerts you immediately with the full conversation. You can try this yourself in the SupportDesk AI demo.

Who owns my data?

You do. Your customer data stays in your own sheet, CRM or account. If you stop the monthly plan, we hand everything over.

Is there a long contract?

No. The monthly plan is month-to-month. Pay the setup fee once, then cancel any time.

Do you work outside Guwahati?

Yes. We meet in person anywhere in Guwahati, and work by video call with businesses across Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh and Sikkim.

How is this different from a ₹999/month chatbot app?

Those apps give you software and leave the setup to you. We build it around your services, prices and booking rules, connect it to your data, and keep improving it every month.
