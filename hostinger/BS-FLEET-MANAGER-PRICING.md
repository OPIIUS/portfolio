# BS Fleet Manager: what to charge, and what it really costs

*Estimates for the Indian market, November 2026. Prices exclude GST.*

## 1. What you are selling

A custom business system built only for BS Car Rentals. It has 12 sections: fleet, live status, bookings with checklists, payments and receipts, outstanding dues, documents with reminders, maintenance, expenses, customers, alerts, reports with Excel/PDF export, and team access with an audit log and backups.

- About 3,500 lines of tested code.
- 90 automated business-rule checks, passing on SQLite and on MySQL/MariaDB.
- Browser-tested on desktop and iPhone.
- The client owns the data and the code, on their own hosting, with no monthly software licence.

**What an agency would quote for this build in India:**

| Who builds it | Typical quote |
|---|---|
| Freelancer, basic version | ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000 |
| Small agency | ₹1,50,000 – ₹3,00,000 |
| Ready-made rental SaaS (instead of custom) | ₹2,500 – ₹8,000 **per month, every month**, and the data sits with them |

## 2. Recommended price for BS Car Rentals

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| **One-time: build, set-up on their Hostinger, data entry of 25 vehicles, 1-hour staff training** | **₹85,000** (accept down to ₹65,000 for a first client / portfolio case) |
| **Care plan (monthly)**: bug fixes, checking backups and cron, small changes (up to 2 hours/month), help on WhatsApp | **₹3,000 / month**, or **₹30,000 / year** paid upfront (2 months free) |
| Bigger changes later (new module, new report) | ₹1,500 / hour, or quoted per job |
| Optional add-on: automatic WhatsApp reminders (connect a WhatsApp Business API provider) | ₹15,000 set-up + the provider's own fees (below) |
| Optional add-on: GPS tracker integration on the live map | ₹20,000 – ₹35,000 set-up, depending on the tracker company's API |

**Payment terms:** 50% advance, 40% on go-live, 10% after 2 weeks of use.

**How to explain the price to the owner:** one missed insurance renewal, one unpaid ₹10,000 rental, or one double-booked car costs more than a month of the care plan. The system tracks all three automatically. Over 3 years, a SaaS at ₹5,000/month costs ₹1,80,000, and they never own it.

## 3. What it really costs you (actual money out)

### To build

| Cost | Amount |
|---|---|
| Your AI subscription for the build period (share of one month) | ~₹1,700 – ₹8,500, depending on your plan |
| Your time: set-up on Hostinger, entering the client's vehicles, training, revisions | ~10 – 15 hours |
| Software licences, libraries, paid APIs | **₹0**: everything is built in |
| **Cash cost to build** | **under ₹10,000** |

### To keep it running (per year)

| Cost | Amount | Who pays |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting | **₹0 extra**: it runs on their existing Hostinger plan, next to the website | Client (already paying) |
| Domain and SSL | **₹0 extra**: uses bscarrental.co.in (free SSL) | Client (already paying) |
| Database, backups, cron, email alerts | **₹0**: included in Hostinger | — |
| Your maintenance time | ~2 – 4 hours / month | You (covered by the ₹3,000 care plan) |
| **Real running cost** | **₹0 extra per month** on their current plan. If they ever need hosting of their own: Hostinger Premium/Business, about ₹2,400 – ₹4,800 / year | |

### Only if they want the optional extras

| Extra | Their running cost |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business API (provider such as Interakt, AiSensy or Gupshup) | ~₹1,000 – ₹3,000 / month plan, plus Meta's per-message charge (utility messages cost well under ₹1 each) |
| GPS trackers | ₹1,500 – ₹3,500 per vehicle, plus ~₹1,000 – ₹1,800 per vehicle per year for the SIM/data plan |
