FAQ

Before you book

How is the price worked out?

Each car has a price per day. A booking is counted in 24-hour days from your pickup time. The price in the booking form is your estimate, and we confirm it on WhatsApp.

Can I take the car outside Assam?

Tell us your route when you book on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

What if the car breaks down?

Call us right away. We act immediately, and if it can't be fixed we may give you a replacement car at no extra cost.

Do you rent bikes too?

Yes, ask us on WhatsApp for the bikes available on your dates.
