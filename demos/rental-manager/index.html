A quick tour

Real screens from the working system, with the made-up demo business.

Step 1

Your fleet in one list

Each car shows if it is available, out with a customer, reserved or in for service, plus its daily rate.

Step 2

Book a car in under a minute

Pick the customer and car, choose the dates, and the price is worked out for you. Take a deposit and an advance if you like.

  • Double bookings are blocked automatically
  • Deposits are kept separate from rent
Step 3

Everything about one rental

Dates, payments, what is still owed and the car's condition before and after. Extend a hire, take a payment or check the car back in from the same page.

  • Late return? You see it straight away
  • Extra charges only if you tick them
Step 4

Who still owes you money

One list of open balances, oldest first, with a one-tap reminder message to the customer.

Step 5

MOT, insurance, tax and servicing

Keep each vehicle's documents and service history in one place and get reminded before anything runs out.

Step 6

Reports you can actually use

Takings, money due, costs and earnings per vehicle for any period. Export to a spreadsheet for your accountant.
