❓ What is actually running here

  1. Real logic, not animations. Every number on these pages is computed while you watch. Change the scoring weights in LeadForge and the ranking changes. Paste your own invoice into DocuParse and it parses that.
  2. They show their working. Each system has an engine log that says what it understood and why it decided what it did — because a client will ask.
  3. They admit what they do not know. The support bot escalates instead of guessing. The research agent tells you which facts only came from one source.
  4. They are built to be re-skinned. Each one has a single config block at the top — hotel, shop, clinic, whichever business is paying.
