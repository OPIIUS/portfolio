FAQ

Good questions

What documents do I need for self-drive?

A valid driving licence held for at least one year, plus one government ID (Aadhaar, passport or voter ID). Minimum age is 21.

How much is the security deposit?

It depends on the car and is shown on your quote. It's collected by UPI at pickup and refunded within 24 hours of return, minus any fuel shortfall, extra km or damage — always with a written breakdown.

What if I drive more than the km limit?

Each car has a free daily km allowance shown on its card. Beyond that, you pay the per-km rate listed — nothing else.

Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 24 hours before pickup. Inside 24 hours, one day's rent is kept from the advance.

Is fuel included?

For self-drive, no — you get a full tank and return it full. For chauffeur rentals and tour packages, fuel is included.

Can you take the car to another state?

Yes for Meghalaya and Arunachal with a driver (we arrange the permits). Self-drive outside the state needs approval at booking.
