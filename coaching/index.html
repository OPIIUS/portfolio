Policies

Refund and exit

Written in plain terms so a parent can read it before paying, not after.

Fee refund policy

  • Cancel within 15 days of the course starting — 90% of tuition fee refunded.
  • Between 16 and 45 days — 60% refunded.
  • Between 46 and 90 days — 25% refunded.
  • After 90 days — no refund of tuition fee.
  • The admission fee is not refundable in any case.
  • Refunds are paid by bank transfer within 21 working days of the written request.
If we cancel or fail to start a batch, 100% of everything paid is returned, including the admission fee.

Easy exit policy

  • A student may leave at any time by giving a written request at the front office.
  • No penalty, and no requirement to complete the course.
  • Original documents are returned within 3 working days.
  • A transfer letter and attendance record are issued on request, free of charge.
  • No student is stopped, and no result or document is withheld, over a fee dispute.
Complaints may be dropped in the complaint box at reception or filed with the District Authority, Kamrup (Metro).
