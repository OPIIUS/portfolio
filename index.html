FAQ

Questions, answered

Is OPIIUS a travel agency?

No. OPIIUS is a marketplace of local agencies. You deal with the agency directly and pay them, at their price.

What does “Verified” mean?

It means OPIIUS has checked the agency's business documents, the owner's identity, their vehicles or equipment, and their location. See our verification standards.

Do I pay anything to OPIIUS?

No. Customers pay no booking fee to OPIIUS.

Are featured agencies better?

Featured agencies pay for top placement and are always labelled. Only Verified agencies can be Featured.

What if something goes wrong?

Tell us on WhatsApp from the agency's page. We follow up with the agency, and unresolved complaints cost them the Verified badge.

Can I list my agency?

Yes. Basic listing is free. See how it works.
