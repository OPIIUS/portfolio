COLOUR — shop by colour, not by category

Every shopping site makes you filter by category first. COLOUR flips it: you pick a colour, and you get clothes that are genuinely that colour. The catch is that retailer colour labels lie, so I wrote a pipeline that pulls each product image and extracts its real dominant colour before the product is allowed in.

Built: 1,022 colour-verified products across men's and women's categories, automated research → import → colour-extraction pipeline, static site deployed to GitHub Pages, packaged as an Android APK with Capacitor.
