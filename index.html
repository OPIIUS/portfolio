How OPIIUS makes money

OPIIUS owns no vehicles. Rental companies keep running their fleets and only pay when OPIIUS brings them a customer. The platform earns a little from both sides of every booking. Commission brings in the most money, and trip protection earns the best margin.

Paid by rental company

Booking commission

12% at launch, 15% at scale

Taken only on the rent of bookings OPIIUS brings. Nothing to list. Founding partners pay 0% for the first 60 days. Zoomcar keeps about 40% from private hosts; OYO charged hotels 20–30%.

Why rental companies will join

  1. They pay only for results. No listing fee, no software switch. If OPIIUS brings nobody, it costs nothing.
  2. Idle days are lost money. A scooter off the road on a Tuesday earns ₹0. Filling even 5 extra days a month is ₹2,000–₹15,000 per vehicle.
  3. Tourists search on Google, not Instagram. A small company cannot rank for "bike rental Guwahati". A platform with 20 companies can.
  4. Money arrives clean. Paid online in advance, payout next morning, deposits and damage disputes handled by OPIIUS.

How to keep the most profit

  1. Take all payments on OPIIUS. Controlling the money stops companies taking repeat riders off-platform and holds float until payout.
  2. Never pay minimum guarantees. OYO grew by guaranteeing hotels income and lost heavily when demand did not match. Pay per booking only.
  3. Push Protect, not discounts. A 45% attach rate on Protect earns more than raising commission by 2 points, and nobody is annoyed by it.
  4. Own the Northeast trip. One-way Guwahati to Shillong, permits for Tawang and Ziro, festival-season pricing. National apps do not do this.
  5. Buy demand cheaply. Pay hostels and hotel desks a referral fee per booking instead of paying for ads.
