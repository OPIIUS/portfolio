Read the full terms
  1. You must be 18 or older with a valid driving licence. Traffic fines during your booking are paid by you.
  2. Your original Voter ID or driving licence stays with us as security. You are responsible for accidental damage, theft or misuse during the booking.
  3. Extensions must be told at least 12 hours before return, otherwise a late fee of ₹300 per hour applies.
  4. Repair costs for damage are paid by you up to ₹50,000. Above that, insurance is claimed and ₹50,000 is still payable.
  5. If the car has a technical or mechanical problem, tell us right away. We help at once and may give a replacement car at no extra cost (not for burnt clutch, burst or flat tyres, or accidental damage).
  6. Returning the car dirty (pan masala stains, cigarette butts) means cleaning charges. Alcohol bottles found in the car mean a ₹1,500 fine.
  7. Fake documents or criminal use of the car lead to legal action and cancellation without refund.
  8. No refund for extra fuel or remaining FASTag balance.
  9. Photos taken at pickup are used as the reference for the car's condition and pickup time.
FAQ

Before you book.

How is the price worked out?

Each car has a price per day. A booking is counted in 24-hour days from your pickup time. The price you see in the booking form is your estimate, and we confirm it on WhatsApp.

Can I take the car to Shillong, Tawang or other states?

Tell us your route when you book on WhatsApp and we'll confirm.

What happens if the car breaks down?

Call us right away. We act immediately, and if it can't be fixed we may give you a replacement car at no extra cost.

Do you rent bikes too?

Yes, ask us on WhatsApp for the bikes available on your dates.
