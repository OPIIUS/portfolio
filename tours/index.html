What “Verified” means

Checked by us, so you don't have to guess.

Before a tour operator earns the OPIIUS Verified badge, we check these. Badges are re-checked every six months and show the date.

Our verification standards
  • Business proof (trade licence, Udyam or GST)
  • Owner's photo ID
  • State tourism registration where it applies
  • At least two past-customer references
  • RC and insurance for the vehicles used on trips

Featured placements are paid and always labelled. Verification is never sold.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I book a tour?

Tell us where, when and how many people. We pass it to a suitable local tour operator, who replies on WhatsApp with a day-by-day plan and price before you commit.

What is usually included?

Hotels, a car with driver and sightseeing are common. Each operator lists what's included and what isn't, such as entry fees, activities and meals.

Do I need a permit?

Arunachal Pradesh needs an Inner Line Permit for Indian visitors, and some areas need extra permits. Your tour operator tells you what's needed and usually arranges it.

When is the best time to visit?

Meghalaya is best from October to May, Kaziranga from November to April when the park is open, and Tawang from March to May and September to November.

Is OPIIUS a travel agency?

No. OPIIUS is a marketplace of local agencies. You deal with the agency directly and pay them, at their price.

Do I pay anything to OPIIUS?

No. Customers pay no booking fee to OPIIUS.

What does “Verified” mean?

OPIIUS has checked the agency's business documents, the owner's identity, their vehicles and their location. See our verification standards.

What if something goes wrong?

Tell us on WhatsApp, quoting your OPIIUS reference. We follow up with the agency, and unresolved complaints cost them the Verified badge.
