Meghalaya

Cloud-covered hills, root bridges grown by hand, and a river so clear the boats look like they float in air. Most trips start in Guwahati and take 3 to 5 days.

Where to go

Places worth the drive

Distances are from Shillong, where most trips are based.

Base

Shillong

The hill capital. Police Bazar, Ward's Lake and the view from Shillong Peak. Umiam Lake sits on the way in from Guwahati.

~55 km

Sohra (Cherrapunji)

Nohkalikai and Seven Sisters falls, Mawsmai cave and some of the heaviest rainfall on earth. Mornings are often foggy.

~20 km

Laitlum Canyons

A ridge that drops into deep green valleys. Best at sunrise or late afternoon, and an easy half-day from Shillong.

~82 km

Dawki & Shnongpdeng

Boating on the Umngot river near the Bangladesh border. The water is clearest from about November to April.

~78 km

Mawlynnong

A tidy village known for its sky walk and the single-decker root bridge at Riwai, usually paired with Dawki.

Trek from Tyrna

Nongriat root bridges

The double-decker living root bridge, reached by a steep walk of about 3,500 steps each way. Plan a full day.

Before you go

Good to know

Carry some cashATMs are common in Shillong but scarce in villages. Many viewpoints, parking lots and boats take cash only.
Drive in daylightHill roads are narrow, fog can roll in fast around Sohra, and monsoon rain can cause slides. Plan to reach your stay before dark.
Check the seasonThe Umngot at Dawki is clearest in the dry months. In the monsoon, waterfalls are at their best but some treks close.
